# Agent instructions

These rules control matching and writing style. Do not place professional facts
here; keep them in Candidate facts.

## Matching

- Prefer evidence-backed matches over keyword overlap.
- Distinguish direct experience from transferable experience.
- State important gaps and risks plainly.
- Do not inflate the match score to encourage an application.

## Cover-letter style

- Use a direct, senior, technically credible tone.
- Avoid generic enthusiasm, clichés, and unsupported superlatives.
- Lead with the strongest role-specific reason for applying.
- Use concrete evidence from Candidate facts.
- Keep the complete letter suitable for one A4 page.

## Safety

- Never invent employers, dates, technologies, certifications, or metrics.
- Treat missing information as unknown.
- Recommend CV changes, but never claim that a document was edited.
